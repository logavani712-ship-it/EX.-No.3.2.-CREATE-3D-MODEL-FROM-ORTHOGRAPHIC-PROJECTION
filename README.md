# EX No.3.2. CREATE 3D MODEL FROM ORTHOGRAPHIC PROJECTION
#### Name:Logavani D
#### Ref no:26018671
#### Date:2.11.2026

To create a 3D model from given orthographic projection.

## REQUIREMENTS:
1. Autodesk fusion 360
2. Windows 10
3. 16 GB of RAM (integrated graphics recommend 1 GB or more)
4. 2.5 Mbps or faster download; 500 Kbps or faster upload 

## PROCEDURE:
1.Arrange the sheet so there is room for the front view, top view, and side view. Draw the required centre lines lightly.

2.Draw the front view with an overall width of 420 mm. Make the base thickness 30 mm.

3.Construct the curved upper portion of the front view with a radius of R110 mm. Draw the central opening 120 mm wide.

4.Project lines from the front view to begin the top view, using the layout shown in the figure.

5.Draw the central portion of the top view 150 mm wide. Form the rounded ends with a radius of R60 mm. The drawing does not label an overall length for this view.

6.Mark the centres of the two end holes 300 mm apart. The centre hole lies midway between them, 150 mm from either end-hole centre.

7.Draw the two end holes as Ø40 mm and the central hole as Ø20 mm. Align all three hole centres on the same centre line.

8.Project the top and front views to construct the side view. Draw it 150 mm wide and 110 mm high.

9.Show the internal feature in the side view at 60 mm high, as indicated in the drawing.

10.Darken visible outlines, use dashed lines for hidden edges, add centre lines, and label the dimensions clearly.
